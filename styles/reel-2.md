# Style: Reel 2 (short-form reference)

Reference: invideo → "The Stress Lie – short cut" → Reel 2
https://invideo.io/@shannons-workspa-310/the-stress-lie-short-cut/notebook/seq-1.3

Studied 2026-10-19: media_probe + timeline captures at 0.3, 1.5, 2.8, 5, 14.7, 29.4, 34.3, 44.8s.
`(?)` = not confirmed from stills.

## Format
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 45.2s
- Talking head (medium shot, black background, lav mic visible) + frequent b-roll and film/stock cutaways

## Captions
- Heavy, extra-bold condensed sans, ALL CAPS, tight letter and word spacing (closest: Montserrat Black / Anton) (?)
- Dark drop shadow behind text
- 1–4 words on screen at a time; the active word is highlighted:
  - Highlight: orange → yellow gradient (e.g. "PERSON", "SHUT", "TOMORROW", "YOU")
  - Other words: white (e.g. "SEE THE", "DAY", "BECAUSE WHAT I KNOW")
- Medium-large: a single word spans ~35–45% of frame width
- Centered horizontally, around 45–55% of frame height (chest level on the speaker, mid-frame on b-roll)
- Word-by-word reveal with pop/scale-in (?)

## Hook / title (0–3s)
- No title card. Three fast cuts in 3s, one caption word each:
  1. 0.0s speaker: "PERSON"
  2. 1.5s film clip (man in suit): "SHUT"
  3. 2.8s red-tinted b-roll in a card on a red background: "OFF"
- The hook is the spoken line itself, captioned big, with fast visual cuts under it

## Cuts and pacing
- Very fast: a new shot every ~1–1.5s in the hook, then every few seconds
- Pauses removed (jump cuts) (?)
- Alternates speaker ↔ b-roll throughout

## B-roll treatment
- Placed as an inset **rounded-corner card** (~90% width) on a black or red background, not full-frame
- Mix of film clips, stock and own footage (cold plunge at 5s)
- Some b-roll graded black & white (eye close-up at 29s)

## Zooms / punch-ins
- Speaker framing switches between medium (0s) and tighter (34s, 44s) → punch-ins on emphasis lines, ~115–125% (?)

## Animations, transitions, graphics
| Element | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Red/yellow duotone flash | 2.8s, 14.7s | Full-frame hot red grade on emphasis beats |
| Rounded-corner b-roll card | throughout | On black, or on red during the red beats |
| B&W b-roll | ~29s | Moody contrast moment |
| Icon/emoji pop (∞) | ~34s | One frame or a short flash (?) |
| Transitions | — | Hard cuts only |

## Audio
- Speech-led with a music bed under it
- Integrated loudness -18.5 LUFS, true peak -1.1 dBTP (quieter than the usual -14 LUFS for Reels)
- Whoosh/hit SFX on cuts (?)

## Length and ending
- 45s
- Ends on the speaker mid-thought with a caption ("DAY"): hard cut, no CTA or end card
