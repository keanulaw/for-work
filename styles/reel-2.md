# Style: Reel 2 (short-form reference)

Reference: invideo → "The Stress Lie – short cut" → Reel 2
https://invideo.io/@shannons-workspa-310/the-stress-lie-short-cut/notebook/seq-1.3

Studied 2026-10-19: media_probe + timeline captures at 0.3, 1.5, 2.8, 5, 14.7, 29.4, 34.3, 44.8s.
`(?)` = not confirmed from stills.

## Format
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 45.2s
- Talking head (medium shot, black background, lav mic visible) + frequent b-roll and film/stock cutaways

## Captions
- Heavy, extra-bold sans, ALL CAPS, tight letter and word spacing (closest: Montserrat Black / Anton) (?)
- Dark drop shadow behind text
- **Animation: hard cut-on, word by word.** No pop, scale, fade or bounce: each word appears instantly at full size on the frame it's spoken.
- Three layouts:
  1. **Single big word** (hook, punchy lines): one word at a time, orange, ~40% frame width
  2. **Building phrase**: words add on as they're spoken ("BUT" → "BUT WHEN I" → "CAN FEEL" on the next line). Upcoming word can show dimmed grey before it's spoken
  3. **Stacked mixed-size block** (emphasis moments): lines of different sizes stacked, e.g. small white "BECAUSE WHAT I KNOW" / big white "IS THAT IF" / medium orange "I'M IN"
- Colors: white for normal words; orange→yellow gradient on key or active words (yellow reads stronger over red beats)
- Position: centered, ~60% height on the speaker (chest), ~50% on b-roll and black beats

## Hook / title (0–3s)
- No title card. Speaker for ~0.8s, then a film clip in a card from ~0.9s, single orange words ("PERSON… WHO'S ABLE TO SHUT OFF"), then a red ink wipe at ~2.7s into a red duotone shot
- The hook is the spoken line itself, captioned big, with fast visual cuts under it

## Cuts and pacing
- Very fast: a new shot every ~1–1.5s in the hook, then every few seconds
- Pauses removed (jump cuts) (?)
- Alternates speaker ↔ b-roll throughout

## B-roll treatment
- Film/stock clips: inset **rounded-corner card** (~90% width) on a black or red background
- Own footage (cold plunge 5s, desert walk 35s): full frame with vignette
- Some b-roll graded black & white (eye close-up at 29s)

## Zooms / punch-ins
- **Not animated.** Punch-ins happen on jump cuts: framing alternates between medium (100%) and tight (~125–135%) from one cut to the next (32s medium → 33s tight → 34s medium-tight)
- No slow push-ins or Ken Burns on the speaker or b-roll

| Element | When | How it animates |
|---|---|---|
| **Red ink/paint wipe** | 2.7s, 14.6s | Grungy torn-edge red shape sweeps in from the top-right over ~3 frames, then the next shot is red/yellow duotone |

| Strong dark vignette | throughout | Static |
| Icon/emoji pop (∞) | ~34s | One-frame flash (?) |
| Other transitions | — | Hard cuts only |

## Audio
- Speech-led with a music bed under it
- Integrated loudness -18.5 LUFS, true peak -1.1 dBTP (quieter than the usual -14 LUFS for Reels)
- Whoosh/hit SFX on cuts (?)

## Length and ending
- 45s
- Ends on the speaker mid-thought with a caption ("DAY"): hard cut, no CTA or end card
